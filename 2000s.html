What Changed in the 2000s?

During the 2000s, websites became more interactive and visually sophisticated. Web designers began separating website content from appearance by using HTML for structure and CSS for styling.

This period also saw the growth of social networking websites, blogging platforms, and websites that allowed users to create and share content.

What Is Web 2.0?

Web 2.0 describes a shift toward websites that encourage interaction, collaboration, and user-generated content.

Instead of simply reading information, users could participate by posting, commenting, sharing, and connecting with other people online.

Popular Design Trends

  • Glossy buttons and rounded corners
  • Gradient backgrounds
  • Drop shadows and colorful icons
  • Blog layouts and comment sections
  • Interactive navigation menus
  • Social networking features

Examples of Web 2.0 Platforms

Platform Year Launched Major Feature
Wikipedia 2001 Collaborative editing
MySpace 2003 Customizable profiles
Facebook 2004 Social networking

Why This Era Matters

The 2000s helped transform the internet from a collection of informational pages into an environment where people could interact and contribute content.
