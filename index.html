Key Terms

Here are some important terms related to the history of web design:

  • HTML: The language used to structure webpage content.
  • CSS: The language used to style webpages.
  • Web 2.0: A period of increased user interaction and content sharing online.
  • Responsive Design: Creating websites that adjust to different screen sizes.
